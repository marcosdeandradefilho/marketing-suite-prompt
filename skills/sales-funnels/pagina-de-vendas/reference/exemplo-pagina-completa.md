copying it. Product (fictional, for the example): **"Doce Lucro"**, a R$197 course that teaches home cooks to

## What to notice (the tuning fork)
- **Zero travessão.** Pauses are periods, commas, parentheses, "e"/"que"/"só que".
- **Sentence rhythm varies:** "Fechou o app." next to a 25-word sentence.
- **Concrete scenes** ("bolo de trezentos reais", "festa junina") instead of "diversos desafios".
- **Benefit-first bullets** with CAPS labels that tease the "como".
- **Mechanism is named** ("Método das 7 Receitas") and explained as a different ORDER, not magic.
- **Proof is a labeled placeholder**, never an invented person.
- **Anchor is honest** (a real R$800 presencial), never an inflated fake total, never "você vai ganhar R$X".
- **Guarantee** states the legal floor honestly then stretches it.
- **Urgency is real** (a turma-specific bonus), not a fake timer.
- **CTA** is 1st person + benefit with risk-killing microcopy under it.
